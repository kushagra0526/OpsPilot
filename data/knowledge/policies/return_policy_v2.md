# NovaMart Return Policy (Version 2)

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy replaces Return Policy version 1 for all return requests received on or after 1 March 2026. It applies to every customer and every order sold through the NovaMart online store. Operators must apply this version and must not use the 30-day blanket window from version 1.

## 2. Return windows by category
Return windows depend on the product category. Electronics (laptops, headphones, earbuds, accessories and other devices) may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Apparel and home goods may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Windows are counted in calendar days from the delivery date recorded on the order.

## 3. Non-returnable items
Grocery and perishable items (including coffee, tea, snacks and other food products) are non-returnable. They cannot be returned or refunded once delivered, including when the customer is unhappy with the taste or freshness. A customer who disputes this decision should be handled under the Escalation SOP.

## 4. Condition of returned items
Apparel and home goods must be unused, with original tags and packaging. Opened electronics are accepted only if they are defective, for example a dead pixel, a faulty battery or a device that does not power on. Unopened electronics in their sealed packaging are accepted for any reason inside the 14-day window. Item condition is inspected by the warehouse when the return arrives; support operators do not decide condition. At the support stage, a return request inside the category window is approved and refunded unless the customer states that a non-defective electronic item has been opened or used.

## 5. Determining eligibility
Eligibility is determined from the order's delivery date, the category of each item being returned and the item condition. If no delivery date is recorded on the order, eligibility cannot be determined and the case must be escalated to a human agent instead of approving or denying the return. Partial returns of individual items from multi-item orders are allowed; each item is assessed against its own category window.

## 6. After an eligible return
Eligible returns are refunded according to the Refund Policy. The operator confirms the outcome to the customer by email, including the amount and the refund method.

## 7. Out-of-window requests
Requests outside the return window are denied. Loyalty members may be offered a goodwill gesture as described in the Loyalty Program policy; this does not reverse the denial.
