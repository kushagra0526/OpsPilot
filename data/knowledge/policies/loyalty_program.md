# NovaMart Loyalty Program

## 1. Overview
The NovaMart Loyalty Program rewards repeat customers. Every customer belongs to one of three tiers: Standard, Gold or Platinum. The tier is recorded on the customer profile and is reviewed every quarter based on the customer's spending over the previous twelve months.

## 2. Tier benefits
Standard members receive order tracking, standard support hours and access to seasonal promotions. Gold members additionally receive priority support and early access to sales. Platinum members receive all Gold benefits plus a dedicated support queue and free express shipping on eligible orders.

## 3. Goodwill gestures
When a return request is denied because it falls outside the return window, operators may offer a goodwill coupon to loyalty members. Gold members may receive a goodwill coupon of up to 10% off their next order. Platinum members may receive a goodwill coupon of up to 15% off. Standard members are not eligible for goodwill coupons. A goodwill coupon does not reverse the denial of the return; it is a courtesy.

## 4. Coupon limits
Coupons above 15% are never allowed without manager approval, regardless of tier or reason. A coupon above the customer's tier limit also requires manager approval. Coupons are valid for 90 days from issue and apply to a single future order.

## 5. Communication
When a goodwill coupon is issued, the operator must explain to the customer why the return was denied and include the coupon code and expiry date in the email.

## 6. Abuse prevention
Goodwill coupons may not be stacked for the same incident. Repeated goodwill requests from the same customer should be reviewed by a support manager.
