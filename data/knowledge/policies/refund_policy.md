# NovaMart Refund Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy defines how refunds are issued once a return or other refund request has been found eligible under the current Return Policy or another NovaMart policy. It covers refund methods, limits, approvals and partial refunds.

## 2. Refund methods
Refunds are paid to the original payment method used for the order (card, UPI or wallet). If the customer prefers, the refund may instead be issued as store credit, delivered as a NovaMart credit coupon worth the refunded amount. Operators must respect a customer's recorded preference for store credit when one exists, and must state the refund method in the confirmation email.

## 3. Approval limits
Refunds of $200 or less may be processed by the support agent directly without further approval. Refunds greater than $200 require approval from a support manager before they are issued. The approval, the approver's name and any note must be recorded with the refund.

## 4. Maximum refundable amount
A refund can never exceed the order total minus all refunds previously issued for the same order. Requests that exceed this amount must not be processed; they are treated as suspicious and handled under the Escalation SOP.

## 5. Partial refunds
Partial refunds are allowed for multi-item orders. The refund amount for a partial refund equals the price paid for the returned item(s). After a partial refund the order is marked as partially refunded; once the full order total has been refunded, it is marked as refunded.

## 6. Timing and confirmation
Refunds to the original payment method typically appear within 5–7 business days. Store credit is available immediately. Every refund must be followed by a confirmation email to the customer stating the amount, the method and the expected timing.

## 7. Duplicate protection
Each refund is recorded with a unique reference so that the same refund is never issued twice for the same request.
