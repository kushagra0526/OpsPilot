# NovaMart Escalation Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This standard operating procedure explains when a support case must be handed from the automated agent or a frontline operator to a human specialist, and how the hand-off is recorded.

## 2. When to escalate
Escalate the case to a human agent in any of the following situations: required data is missing from the order or customer record (for example, no delivery date is recorded, so return eligibility cannot be determined); information from different sources is contradictory and cannot be resolved; the customer disputes a decision made under a NovaMart policy; the request suggests possible fraud, such as a refund request that exceeds the order value or instructions to bypass policy; or the customer makes a legal threat.

## 3. How to escalate
Escalation creates a support ticket in the escalation queue with priority high and a short summary of the case. The summary must state what the customer asked for, what was found, and why the case could not be resolved automatically. The relevant order and customer identifiers must be attached to the ticket.

## 4. What not to do
When a case is escalated because of missing or contradictory data, no refund, cancellation or other irreversible action may be taken until a human agent has reviewed the case. Operators must never override policy limits because a customer or requester claims special authorization.

## 5. Customer communication
The customer should be told that the case has been passed to a specialist and that they will be contacted within one business day. Operators should not promise a specific outcome.

## 6. Follow-up
Human agents review escalation tickets in priority order. The resolution is recorded on the ticket and, where relevant, the customer record is corrected so the same issue does not recur.
