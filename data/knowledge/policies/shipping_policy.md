# NovaMart Shipping Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy covers delivery promises, late deliveries and cancellations for orders shipped by NovaMart within its delivery network.

## 2. Delivery promise
The standard delivery promise is 7 calendar days from the order date. The promised delivery date is shown to the customer at checkout and in the order confirmation email. Orders are tracked from placement through shipping to delivery, and customers can follow the status in their account.

## 3. Late deliveries
If an order is more than 10 days past its promised delivery date and has not been delivered, the operator must offer the customer a 10% coupon as an apology and open a shipping-investigation ticket with the carrier team. The coupon and the ticket are independent actions and may be completed together. The customer must then be informed by email that the investigation has been opened and that the coupon has been issued. Orders that are late by 10 days or fewer should receive a status update only.

## 4. Cancellations
Orders that have not yet shipped (status placed) may be cancelled at the customer's request. Cancellation is irreversible: a cancelled order cannot be reinstated and must be placed again. Because it is irreversible, a cancellation must be approved by a supervisor before it is executed. Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled; the customer must wait for delivery and then follow the Return Policy.

## 5. Address changes
Delivery address changes are possible only before the order ships. After shipping, the address cannot be changed.

## 6. Lost parcels
A parcel is considered lost when the shipping investigation confirms it. Lost parcels are refunded in full or replaced, at the customer's choice, according to the Refund Policy.
