# NovaMart Return Policy (Version 1)

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy describes how NovaMart handles product returns for orders placed through the NovaMart online store. It applies to every customer, regardless of loyalty tier, and to every product category sold by NovaMart. Support operators must apply this policy consistently and record the outcome of every return request in the order history.

## 2. Return window
All products are returnable within 30 days of delivery. The 30-day window is counted in calendar days starting from the delivery date recorded on the order. Requests received after the window has closed are not eligible for a return under this policy. The same 30-day window applies to electronics, apparel, home goods and grocery items.

## 3. Condition of returned items
Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging with all accessories, manuals and tags included. Items that show signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing components may be refused at the warehouse. Operators should remind customers to keep proof of delivery and the original packaging until the return is complete.

## 4. Return process
The customer contacts NovaMart support with the order number and a short description of the reason for the return. The operator confirms that the order was delivered within the last 30 days, generates a return authorization, and emails the customer the return instructions. Once the warehouse receives and inspects the item, the refund is processed according to the Refund Policy.

## 5. Exceptions
Items damaged in transit or delivered incorrectly may be returned at any time within the 30-day window free of charge, and NovaMart covers the return shipping cost. Any request outside this policy must be escalated to a support manager, who decides case by case and documents the decision on the ticket.

## 6. Policy changes
NovaMart may update this policy. When a new version takes effect, it replaces this version for all return requests received on or after the new effective date. Operators must always apply the latest effective version.
